What we believe

The same five things guide everything Jodax ships. They're not slogans — they're how the work actually gets done.

  • Simple beats clever. Every feature earns its place, or it doesn't ship.
  • Honest, never deceptive. No dark patterns, no silent surprises.
  • The details are the product. A tool that works but feels wrong isn't finished.
  • Respect your control. Your business, your levers — we get out of the way.
  • We do the last 20%. The part where it actually ships and holds up.

How Sam came to be

Every good tool has a backstory. Sam's involves two that came before.

Attempt one — Sally. The first try. Ambitious, stubborn, and ultimately a house of cards — it worked, until you changed anything, and then nothing worked. A hundred hours went in. Lessons were learned the hard way.

Attempt two — Sara. A rethink from the ground up, with strategy fixed before a line was written. Stable in an afternoon, and accepted on the first try. A featherweight, but a real one — and proof the approach was right.

Attempt three — Sam. The real contender. Everything learned from the first two, built properly: full-featured, tested three ways, polished, and finished down to the last detail. The one you came here about.

Third time lucky? Not luck. Just refusing to stop at 80%.

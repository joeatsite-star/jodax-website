🔥 The headline act

Storewide sales

Sam doesn't just reward big carts — it can run a flat, instant sale across your whole store. Set a threshold of $0 and the offer fires the moment a visitor arrives, discount applied automatically. Perfect for Black Friday, end-of-financial-year, clearance and flash sales.

Storewide sale Free

The simple storewide sale

One flat discount across everything, live the instant someone lands on your store. The digital version of a big "20% OFF EVERYTHING" sign in the window. No spend required.

The settings

  1. Create a Base card
  2. Set Minimum cart value to 0
  3. Set Discount type to Percentage, and Discount % to 20 (or your number)
  4. In the Reward message, write your sale announcement (see below). Leave intro and teaser blank — they don't show on a $0 sale
  5. Running it for a set period? Add Start / End dates and tick Show countdown timer for the "Ends in…" counter
  6. Save, and confirm the storewide-sale prompt

Copy this message

Reward message
💰💰💰⭐⭐⭐ Black Friday sale 20% off everything storewide! today only ⭐⭐⭐💰💰💰

Intro & teaser: leave blank.

Only % or free shipping. A $0 sale can use a percentage or free-shipping reward. Fixed-amount and free-gift rewards aren't allowed at $0 (they'd give value away to every visitor for nothing).

The country-specific sale

A storewide sale shown only where you want it. An Australian end-of-financial-year sale that greets shoppers in Australia, while everyone else sees your store as normal. Set one country or several.

The settings

  1. Build a storewide sale Base card — threshold 0, Percentage 20
  2. In Countries, tick Australia (or several countries)
  3. Write your Reward message for that audience (see below)
  4. Add Start / End dates to run it for the sale period, and tick Show countdown timer for the "Ends in…" counter
  5. Save, and confirm the storewide-sale prompt

Copy this message

Reward message
💰 It's our Australian end of financial year sale! 20% off everything storewide! today only 💰

Intro & teaser: leave blank.

One sale at a time. Sam runs one active ladder at a time, so you can't run a different country-locked sale in each market simultaneously. Set the countries you want on your one live sale. (This is by design — it keeps which offer is showing unambiguous.)

⚠️ Watch your discount math

Be careful stacking a big tier discount on top of a sale. If the higher reward is too steep, a bigger cart can actually earn you less than a smaller one — and in the worst case, discount you below cost.

Example: a $399 cart at 20% off = you collect $319. A $400 cart at 40% off = you collect $240. The shopper spent $1 more, but you earned $79 less on that sale. Always check the total still makes sense at the point the tier unlocks.

⚠️ Fixed-amount pitfalls — read these
  • 1. Always state your currency. Unlike a percentage, a fixed amount does not auto-convert. A shopper seeing "$10 off" abroad doesn't know if that's USD, AUD, CAD… Write "USD $10.00" so it's unambiguous. Your {remaining} token still shows in their currency — it's only the fixed reward figure you must label.
  • 2. Set the threshold high enough. $10 off a $100 cart is 10%. But $10 off a $30 cart is 33%. Keep the threshold well above the reward so you're not giving away too much on small carts.
  • 3. Not for storewide sales. A fixed amount can't be used on a $0 storewide sale (it'd hand cash to every visitor). Sam blocks that — use percentage or free shipping for a $0 sale.
The delight move

The surprise thankyou

Not every reward needs a countdown. Set a threshold, leave the intro and teaser blank, and let a free gift plus free shipping simply appear at the finish line — an unexpected thankyou that turns a good order into a great memory.

Sam Gold

Free gift + free shipping, no fanfare

A single base card that stacks two rewards — a free gift and free shipping — with no intro or teaser. The shopper isn't chased toward it; they just cross the threshold and get a warm surprise. Because intro and teaser are left blank, only the thankyou shows.

The settings

  1. Create a Base card, Minimum cart value 100
  2. Turn on Free shipping as the reward
  3. Turn on Free gift and pick the product (set its price to $0.00, keep it unlisted so it's not sold separately)
  4. Leave Intro and Teaser blank — this is a surprise
  5. Write only the Reward message (below)

Copy this message

Reward message
🎁💗 Hi there! Please accept a free gift and free shipping on us as thanks for shopping 😊

Intro & teaser: leave blank.

Two rewards, one card. A gift rides alongside any reward (here, free shipping) — just remember the ladder allows only one gift. The gift product must exist in your catalog at $0.00; draft products can't be added to a cart.

The result

🎁 How to create your free gift product
  • 1. Make the product in Shopify. Go to Shopify admin → Products → Add product. Give it a name (e.g. "Free gift — baseball cap") and an image, exactly like any other product.
  • 2. Price it $0.00. Set the price to zero so the shopper is charged nothing when it's added. This is what makes it "free."
  • 3. Set status to Unlisted. In the product's status, choose Unlisted (not Draft). Unlisted means the app can add it to the cart, but it won't show up in your storefront or search for shoppers to buy on its own. Draft won't work — draft products can't be added to a cart.
  • 4. Give it some stock. Add inventory (even a large number) so Sam can add it to carts — a $0 item with zero stock still can't be added.
  • 5. Attach it in Sam. Back on your card, turn on Free gift and pick your $0.00 product from the list. Save — done.

The Ladder Golden Rules

The full logic of how ladders work, laid out plainly. Keep these in mind and your offers will always behave.

  • One ladder runs at a time. You can build a whole library of ladders, but only one is ever live — this keeps which offer is showing unambiguous.
  • Base cards hold the settings. Only a Base card sets the ladder's name, dates, countries, and pause. It's the foundation everything else attaches to.
  • Tier cards attach to a Base. When you create a Tier, you pick its Base by name to join that ladder. A Tier can't exist on its own.
  • Tiers inherit dates & countries. Tier cards can't set their own schedule or country targeting — they follow whatever their Base is set to.
  • Tiers can be moved. Edit a Tier and select a different Base name to move it onto another ladder.
  • The Base is the lowest rung. The Base always has the lowest threshold; every Tier must be higher.
  • Rewards climb with spend. A higher rung can't give a smaller reward than a lower one — the ladder only ever goes up.
  • One discount type per ladder. All percentage, or all fixed, or all free shipping — not mixed. (A free gift can be added on top, as long as there's only one gift in the whole ladder.)
  • A $0 Base is a storewide sale. Set the Base threshold to $0 and it fires instantly for every visitor. At $0, only percentage or free-shipping rewards are allowed.
  • Word your banners with {remaining}, not fixed amounts. Typing {remaining} in a message shows the exact amount left to reach the reward — and it displays in each shopper's own currency automatically, so it's always right. Your store may be viewed in different countries and currencies, so if you must write an actual amount, always state your store's currency: "Just {remaining} to win $10 USD off your cart!"
  • Free vs Sam Gold. Free runs one always-active ladder (up to 4 cards). Sam Gold lets you build multiple ladders (up to 20 cards) and schedule them — one always-active, plus dated ladders in their own windows — and unlocks country targeting, free gifts and countdowns. Only one ladder is ever live at a moment.

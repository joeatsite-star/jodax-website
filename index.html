How we build

We build tools the way we'd want them built for us.

We build focused software — carefully, honestly, and finished properly. These aren't slogans; they're how we work, and you can see them in everything we ship. Led by Joe on design & quality, Dax on research & support, and some important help from our electronic partners — we build lean, and we build things we'd want to use ourselves.

01 · Simple beats clever

Every feature earns its place, or it doesn't ship. Given a fragile-but-impressive way and a plain-but-solid way, we take the plain one. Complexity is a cost, not a feature.

02 · Honest, never deceptive

Our tools do exactly what we say — no dark patterns, no silent surprises. If something can't be done cleanly, we'll tell you rather than fake it.

03 · The details are the product

We test on real devices, sweat the wording, and design things to look like they belong where they live. A tool that works but feels wrong isn't finished.

04 · Respect your control

It's your business, your platform, your decisions. Our tools give you the levers and get out of your way — clear, predictable, and yours to command.

05 · We do the last 20%

Anyone can get something 80% working. We're here for the part where it actually ships, holds up, and doesn't embarrass you six months later — the polish, the edge cases, the finish.

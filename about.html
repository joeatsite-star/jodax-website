About Jodax

We build tools the way we'd want them built for us.

Jodax is a focused software studio making genuinely useful tools and applications. We sweat the details, test on real devices, and design things to look and run great in whatever application is required.

We build focused software — and we build it carefully, honestly, and finished properly.

These aren't slogans. They're how we work, and you can see them in everything we ship.

01

Simple beats clever

Every feature earns its place, or it doesn't ship. When there's a fragile-but-impressive way and a plain-but-solid way, we take the plain one. Complexity is a cost, not a feature.

02

Honest, never deceptive

Our tools do exactly what we say — no dark patterns, no silent surprises, nothing that misleads you or the people you serve. If something can't be done cleanly, we'll tell you rather than fake it.

03

The details are the product

We test on real devices, sweat the wording, and design things to look like they belong where they live. A tool that works but feels wrong isn't finished.

04

Respect your control

It's your business, your platform, your decisions. Our tools give you the levers and get out of your way — clear, predictable, and yours to command.

05

We do the last 20%

Anyone can get something 80% working. We're here for the part where it actually ships, holds up, and doesn't embarrass you six months later — the polish, the edge cases, the finish.

A small studio, building carefully

Led by Joe on design & quality control, Dax on concept research & customer support, and some important assistance from our electronic partners. We build lean, we build carefully, and we build things we'd want to use ourselves. Build it right, ready and reliable. The first time.

You can see how we work in our tools for Shopify merchants — for example Sam — Cart Offers & Rewards, built on exactly these principles: tested on real devices, designed to blend into any store, and finished down to the last detail. More tools are on the way.

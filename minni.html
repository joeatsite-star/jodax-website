Minni File Compressor

An honest file compressor for Android. Shrink your videos, photos and PDFs down to the size you need — hit a target like 10 MB, or pick a quality — and Minni tells you the truth when a file can't go smaller. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking.

Available on Google Play

What Minni does

🎯

Compress to a size

Hit a target like 10, 16 or 25 MB — or set your own custom size. Perfect for upload and attachment limits.

🎚️

Or compress by quality

Prefer to think in quality? Pick High, Balanced or Small and Minni shrinks the file to match.

🎬

Video, photos and PDFs

Shrink the common files on your phone — MP4 and MOV video, JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC photos, and PDFs.

🧮

Honest when it can't

If a file won't fit your target, Minni says so plainly and gets it as small as it honestly can — never silent, mushy guesswork.

🗂️

Keeps your original

Minni makes a new, smaller copy and never touches your original file. Nothing to lose.

📤

Share or save, named by size

Send straight to another app or save to your device — each file named with its new size, like clip_minni_9.8MB.mp4.

🔒

Private, on your device

Everything happens offline on your phone. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking — and no access to your photos or storage.

Free, and honest about it

Compressing one file at a time is free — every type, no ads, no account, no nonsense. Shrink as many files as you like, one at a time.

Batch compression

Need to shrink a whole pile at once? Unlock Batch through the Google Play Store — a one-time purchase, no subscription. Pick your files, set one target for all of them, and Minni works through the lot.

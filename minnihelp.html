Help & FAQ

How to compress a file

  1. Open Minni and tap Pick a file to compress.
  2. Choose the video, photo or PDF you want to shrink.
  3. Choose how small you want it: To a size (a target like 10 MB) or By quality (High, Balanced or Small).
  4. Tap Compress.
  5. When it's done, tap Share to send it elsewhere, or Save to device.

How to compress to a specific size

Choose To a size, then pick a limit — 10, 16 or 25 MB — or tap Custom for your own. Minni aims to land just under it. For video and photos you'll see an estimate before you start; for PDFs the real size is shown after, since PDF sizes are hard to predict up front.

How to compress by quality

Prefer to think in quality instead of a number? Choose By quality and pick High, Balanced or Small. Minni shrinks the file to that level and shows you the result.

How to compress several files at once (Batch)

Batch compression is a one-time paid upgrade — no subscription. Once unlocked through the Google Play Store, tap Compress several at once, pick your files, set one target for all of them, and let Minni work through the lot. You'll see overall progress and a per-file list, and you can Share or Save them all together.

FAQ

Is Minni free?
Yes — compressing one file at a time is completely free, with no ads and no account required. Batch compression is an optional one-time upgrade. No subscription, ever.
What can Minni compress?
Videos, photos and PDFs. For video and photos it handles the common formats your phone already uses — like MP4, MOV, JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC. If it meets a file it can't read, it tells you rather than guessing.
What's the difference between "To a size" and "By quality"?
To a size aims to get a file under a limit you choose — handy for upload caps. By quality lets you pick High, Balanced or Small and shrinks to that level instead.
What if a file can't hit my target size?
Minni gets it as small as it honestly can without wrecking the quality, then tells you plainly — and suggests trimming or splitting if you need it smaller still. It never silently turns your file to mush just to hit a number.
Does Minni keep my original?
Always. Minni makes a new, compressed copy and never touches your original file.
Where do my compressed files go?
You can Share them straight to another app, or Save them to your device. Each file is named with its new size — like clip_minni_9.8MB.mp4 — so the small one is easy to spot.
Why did Minni keep my original instead of shrinking it?
If compressing would actually make the file bigger, or a PDF is mostly text (where shrinking would just blur it), Minni keeps your original so you don't lose anything for no gain.
How do I get Batch compression?
Unlock it once through the Google Play Store. It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.
Does Minni collect my data or need the internet?
No. Everything happens on your device, offline. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking.

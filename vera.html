Vera Audio Converter

A clean, straightforward audio converter for Android. Convert your audio files between formats, extract audio from video, and get your files exactly where you want them — with no ads, no sign-up, and no clutter.

Available on Google Play

What Vera does

🔄

Convert between 7 formats

MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, Opus, AAC, and ALAC (Apple Lossless) — convert your audio to whichever you need.

🎬

Extract audio from video

Pull the sound out of MP4, MOV, MKV, and other common video files, straight to the audio format you choose.

🎚️

Choose your quality

Pick the bitrate — 128, 192, 256, or 320 kbps — for compressed formats.

🎵

Save straight to Music

Converted files go right where your music player can find them, ready to play.

🏷️

Keep your track details

Artist, title, and album information is carried over during conversion.

⏪

Reverse a track

Flip any audio to play backwards — for creative projects, practice, or fun.

✂️

Trim to any section

Cut a clip to the start and end times you want. Handy for making your own ringtones — note the times in your music player, enter them in Vera, and save the section.

Free, and honest about it

Single-file conversion is free — all formats, no ads, no account, no nonsense. Convert one file at a time, as much as you like.

Batch conversion

Need to convert a whole folder at once? Upgrade Vera through the Google Play Store to unlock batch conversion — select many files and convert them all in one go.

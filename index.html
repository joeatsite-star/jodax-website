How it works

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

STEP 01

Set a reward ladder

Pick a spending threshold and the reward it unlocks — a discount, free shipping, or a free gift.

STEP 02

Sam shows the offer

A banner tells each shopper how close they are and what they'll unlock — styled to match your store.

STEP 03

The reward applies itself

When they qualify, Sam applies the discount automatically at checkout. Nothing else to configure.

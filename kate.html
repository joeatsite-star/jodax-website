What Kate does

🖼️

Any image into a pattern

Photos, cartoons, drawings, artwork — Kate matches your image to real drill colours using perceptual (CIELAB) colour matching, for true-to-life results.

💎

The full DMC palette

All 454 DMC colours are built in, so every drill in your pattern maps to a real colour code you can buy.

✨

Automatic cleanup

Smart confetti reduction tidies away scattered stray drills, so your pattern is calmer and far more pleasant to work.

🖌️

Edit by hand

Zoom right in and recolour individual cells — fix a stray colour, sharpen an edge, make it exactly how you want.

🖨️

Two ways to print

A clear, readable working chart to follow as you go — or a true-size print-at-home kit to lay your drills on directly.

📄

Print-ready PDF

Clear symbols, a colour legend, and drill counts for every colour — printed true to scale, so you know exactly what to order.

📚

Your pattern library

Save, rename, and reopen your patterns any time. Your work is kept safe on your device.

🔒

Yours, offline

No account, no ads, and nothing sent anywhere. Your images and patterns never leave your device.

Free to download and try — unlock to print.

Pick an image, build your pattern, tune the colours, and fine-tune it by hand — all free, so you see exactly how it'll turn out. When you're ready, a one-time unlock (no subscription, ever) lets you print and export your finished patterns.

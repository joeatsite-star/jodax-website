Everything Sam does

A lot of app, for a little money.

Sam turns your cart into a reward ladder — shoppers see how close they are to the next perk, and Sam applies it automatically when they get there. Here's the full toolkit.

Build the offer

Free

The core of Sam: set spending thresholds, and choose what each one unlocks. Simple enough for your first offer in minutes, deep enough to build a full reward ladder.

🎯

Reward ladders

Set a spending threshold and the reward it unlocks. Shoppers see exactly how much more to spend to get there — the nudge that lifts cart totals.

🔥

Storewide sales

Set a threshold of $0 and your offer becomes an instant storewide sale — the discount shows and applies the moment a visitor arrives. Perfect for Black Friday, clearance, and seasonal sales.

🪜

Tiers & multiple thresholds

Stack several rungs on one ladder — spend $50 for one reward, $100 for a bigger one. Bigger carts unlock better perks, and Sam always shows the next step up.

🗂️

Base & tier cards

Organise offers with a base card and its tiers, so a single ladder can carry a whole progression. Create and save up to 20 different offers, and run them at your discretion.

⏸️

Enable, disable, pause

Turn any offer on or off in a click. Nothing is ever locked in — you're always in control of what's live.

Choose the reward

Four kinds of reward, and Sam applies them for you. Percentage, fixed-amount, and free-shipping discounts apply automatically at checkout — no separate Shopify discount to set up.

⚡

Percentage discount Free

Take a percentage off the whole cart when the shopper qualifies — applied automatically at checkout.

💵

Fixed-amount discount Free

Knock a set dollar amount off the order the moment the threshold is reached. No codes, no manual steps.

🚚

Free shipping Free

Unlock free shipping at a spending level you choose — Sam removes the shipping cost at checkout when they get there.

🎁

Free gift Gold

Reward a full cart with a free product. Pick the gift from your own catalogue, and Sam adds it as the unlocked perk.

Show it beautifully

Sam's storefront banner does the talking — and it's built to look like part of your store, not a widget bolted on top.

📊

Three-state banner

An intro when the cart is empty-ish, a teaser as they get close, and a reward message when they've unlocked it — with a progress bar that fills as they add to cart.

🎨

Matches your store

Sam inherits your theme's fonts automatically — or point it at a specific theme font (like your Header, Body, or Navigation style) for Sam to read and apply. Then set the banner and text colours to match your palette, or use brighter eye-catching colours of your choice.

✍️

Your words, your emoji

Write each message yourself, with a built-in emoji picker and a preview frame to view and tweak it. Say it in your store's voice.

🛒

Floating cart badge

Shopify's built-in cart badge shows shoppers how many items are in their cart. Sam lets you leave it on or off — a small storefront convenience alongside the banner.

Control when & where

Gold

Fine-tune exactly who sees an offer and when it runs — for seasonal campaigns, regional promotions, and time-limited pushes.

📅

Scheduling

Give an offer a start and end date so seasonal promotions switch themselves on and off. Set it once and forget it.

🌍

Country targeting

Show an offer only to shoppers in the countries you choose — run a promotion in one market without touching the rest.

⏳

Countdown urgency

Add a countdown so shoppers know a deal won't last — the nudge that turns "maybe later" into "add to cart now."

🎛️

Cart monitoring controls

Choose which pages the banner appears on and how responsively it updates, so it behaves exactly the way your store needs.

Built right

The details that don't show up in a feature list but decide whether an app is a pleasure or a headache.

📱

Works everywhere

Desktop and mobile, tested on real devices. The admin works on your phone; the banner looks right on your shoppers' phones.

🔒

No customer data collected

Sam works from cart totals and your shop's settings. It doesn't gather your customers' personal information — less risk, less to worry about.

⚙️

Automatic, not manual

The reward applies itself at checkout. No discount codes to hand out, no separate Shopify discount to keep in sync.

🧮

Honest by design

Sam shows shoppers real progress toward real rewards. No dark patterns, no fake urgency you didn't set — it does exactly what you tell it.

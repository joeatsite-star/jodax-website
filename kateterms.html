🧮 The short version

Kate runs entirely on your device. We don't collect your data, show ads, or require an account — your images and patterns never leave your phone. Kate is free to download and try; a one-time purchase (no subscription) unlocks printing and exporting. That's it.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Nothing. Kate has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no ads. We don't collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

Your images and patterns

Everything happens on your device. The images you import and the patterns you create stay on your phone or tablet — they are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

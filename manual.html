1. The ladder system

The ladder is Sam's core engine. Shoppers unlock rewards as their cart total climbs past thresholds you set — the higher they spend, the better the reward.

Thresholds & rungs

Each rung of a ladder is a spending threshold and the reward it unlocks. Set a threshold (say $75), pick what it gives (a discount, free shipping, or a gift), and Sam shows shoppers exactly how far they are from reaching it.

Base cards and tier cards

  • A base card is the anchor of a ladder. It owns the ladder's identity, its name, and (on Sam Gold) its dates and country targeting.
  • Tier cards are extra rungs that attach to a base — higher thresholds unlocking bigger rewards. Tiers inherit the base's schedule and targeting.
  • Sam groups tiers under their base and renders them as one ladder, read left-to-right (base → higher tiers).

How the reward is chosen

Sam always gives a cart the best reward it currently qualifies for — it "snaps" to the highest rung the cart has reached. As shoppers add more, they move up automatically; if they remove items, they move back down. It's always live and always accurate.

The $0 storewide sale: set a base card's threshold to $0 and it becomes an instant storewide sale — the reward shows and applies the moment a visitor arrives, with no minimum spend. You can still stack a tier on top (e.g. 20% off everything, plus 40% at $200). A $0 sale uses a percentage or free-shipping reward; on a $0 base only the reward message shows (intro and teaser don't apply). See the Recipes page for sale recipes.
Free vs Gold: the free plan runs one always-active ladder. Sam Gold lets you build multiple ladders and schedule them — one always-active, plus dated ladders that run in their own windows. Either way, exactly one ladder is live at a time — Sam enforces this so banners and rewards never conflict.

2. Reward types

Choose what each rung unlocks. There are four reward types (plus a message-only option). Percentage, fixed-amount and free-shipping rewards apply automatically at checkout — no separate Shopify discount to create.

Percentage discount

Takes a percentage off the cart (e.g. 10% off) when the shopper qualifies. A percentage isn't tied to any currency — 10% is 10% for every shopper, everywhere — so you can write it freely in your banner messages without worrying about conversion.

Free shipping

Removes the shipping cost at checkout once the threshold is reached. Simply choose free shipping as the reward and set the spend required — Sam handles the rest. Like a percentage, it carries no currency figure, so it's safe to mention plainly in your banner.

Free gift Sam Gold

Automatically offers a product from your catalogue as the reward. To set one up: create the gift product in Shopify, set its price to $0.00 and its status to Unlisted (Draft products can't be added to a cart), then choose it with Choose gift product in the reward settings. When the shopper qualifies, they can add it to their cart for free.

Fixed-amount discount

Takes a set amount off the cart total (e.g. $50 off). Sam handles this in your shop's own currency. If a shopper is browsing in a different currency, the amount isn't shown converted on the banner — but it is automatically converted at checkout, so the discount they receive is correct for their currency.

Important — fixed amounts in your banner: because your banner text is static, always state the currency when you mention a fixed amount, e.g. "$50 USD off when you spend $1000 USD." That way a shopper in another currency understands the figure is in your shop's currency (and knows it'll be converted for them at checkout). See the banner section for more on writing amounts.

Message-only rung

A rung that shows a message with no reward attached — useful for pure encouragement ("Almost at free shipping!") or to add an earlier teaser prompt.

Automatic by design: for percentage, fixed and free-shipping rewards, Sam applies the discount itself at checkout. There are no codes to hand out and nothing to keep in sync.

3. The storefront banner

The banner is what shoppers see. It changes as they move up the ladder, so the message always matches where they are.

The three states

  • Intro — the first thing shoppers see, introducing the offer.
  • Teaser — shown partway there, nudging them toward the reward. Sam can automatically show a mid-point teaser to shoppers who are on their way.
  • Reward — the reward message, shown once they've unlocked it.

Progress bar & rewards box

A progress bar fills as the cart grows, giving shoppers a visual sense of how close they are. When they qualify, the rewards box displays what they've earned with an "Earned:" label.

You write each message yourself, with a built-in emoji picker and a preview frame to view and tweak it. Message fields have character limits with a live counter so your text always fits.

Writing amounts: use {remaining}

When you want to tell shoppers how much more to spend, type {remaining} into your message instead of a fixed number. Sam replaces it live with the exact amount left to reach the reward — for example, "Spend {remaining} more to unlock free shipping!" might show as "Spend $12.00 more...".

Best of all, {remaining} shows in each shopper's own store currency automatically. That's why it's worth avoiding hard currency figures in your banners: if you write "Spend $20 more", a shopper browsing in euros or pounds sees a figure that doesn't match their cart. Let {remaining} do the maths, and your message reads correctly for everyone, everywhere.

Rule of thumb: write the idea ("you're almost there!") and let {remaining} handle the number. Reward amounts are always shown in your shop's currency — see Currency handling.
Using a fixed-amount reward? If your banner mentions the discount itself (e.g. "$50 off"), state the currency — write "$50 USD off", not just "$50 off". Banner text is static and doesn't convert, so naming the currency tells shoppers in other countries the figure is in your shop's currency (Sam converts the actual discount for them at checkout). Full detail in Reward types.

4. Styling & fonts

Sam is built to look like part of your store, not a widget bolted on top. Styling is set in the Shopify theme editor.

Fonts

Sam inherits your theme's fonts automatically. If you'd like more control, you can point Sam at a specific theme font — for example your Header, Body, or Navigation font style — and Sam will read that setting and apply it, so the banner text matches your store exactly.

Colours

Set the banner background and text colours, the progress bar, and the badge to match your palette — or use brighter, eye-catching colours of your choice to make the offer stand out.

Where: styling lives in Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → Sam Cart Monitor. Remember to Save in the theme editor after changing it.

5. Floating cart badge

A small cart badge that shows how many items are currently in the cart.

This is Shopify's built-in floating cart badge — a simple item counter that sits on your storefront. You can choose to show or hide it, and when it's on, it displays the number of items in the shopper's cart. It's a standard storefront convenience rather than a Sam reward feature, but you'll find the option among the settings, so it's covered here for completeness.

6. Currency handling

Sam is honest about money. It shows values in your shop's currency and applies discounts correctly across currencies.

  • Currency honesty — amounts display in the shop's own currency, with no fake or misleading conversions.
  • Cross-currency correctness — when a shopper checks out in a different presentment currency, Sam's discounts still apply correctly.
Good to know: any reward amount you set is shown and applied in your shop's own currency. Sam won't invent an exchange rate or quietly convert it into something else — what you set is what your shoppers see, honestly and consistently.

7. Scheduling Sam Gold

Run offers on a timetable — perfect for seasonal sales and limited-time promotions.

  • Dated ladders — give a ladder a start and end date, and it switches itself on and off automatically.
  • Always-active ladders — run indefinitely (this is how the free plan's single ladder works).
  • Countdown timer — show shoppers the time remaining on a dated offer, adding urgency.
  • Pause scheduled ladders — a global switch to pause all dated ladders at once; Sam falls back to an always-active ladder so your store is never left without an offer.
Cards must be enabled to run. New cards are created disabled by default — enable a card to bring it into play. You can have an always-active card enabled alongside several dated cards: the always-active one is your everyday offer, and whenever a dated card's window is live, it takes over. When the dated window ends, Sam falls back to the always-active card, so your store is never left without an offer.
No overlaps: Sam prevents two dated ladders from covering the same dates, so there's never a conflict about which offer is live.

8. Country targeting Sam Gold

Show an offer only to shoppers in the countries you choose — run a promotion in one market without touching the rest.

  • Country allowlist — pick the countries an offer applies to; shoppers elsewhere simply don't see it.
  • Set on the base, inherited by tiers — you set targeting once on the base card and the whole ladder follows.
  • Fails open — if a shopper's country can't be determined, Sam still shows the offer rather than hiding it, so you never lose a sale to uncertainty.

9. The rules that keep offers sane

Sam has a set of built-in guardrails. If an offer breaks one, Sam tells you with a clear message when you save — so you can't accidentally publish something contradictory.

  • The base is the lowest threshold — the base card always sits at the bottom of its ladder; tiers go above it.
  • Ladder names are unique — no two ladders share a name, so they're easy to tell apart.
  • One live undated ladder — exactly one always-active base is enabled at a time.
  • Dated windows don't overlap — scheduled ladders can't cover the same dates.
  • One reward family per ladder — a single ladder sticks to one kind of reward, so the progression is consistent.
  • Rewards go up with spend — higher thresholds must give better rewards; Sam won't let a bigger spend unlock a smaller reward.
  • One gift per ladder — at most one free-gift rung on a ladder.
Clear errors: when a rule is broken, Sam shows a plain-language message (as a pop-up toast, visible wherever you're scrolled) telling you exactly what to fix.

10. Plans & upgrading

Everything you need to run real reward ladders is free. Sam Gold unlocks more ladders, more cards, and the advanced features.

Free

  • One always-active ladder, up to 4 cards (1 base + 3 tiers)
  • Percentage, fixed-amount and free-shipping rewards
  • The full banner, styling, progress bar and cart badge

Sam Gold

  • Multiple ladders, up to 20 cards
  • All reward types, including free gifts
  • Scheduling, countdowns, and country targeting

Building a library, and downgrading

You can build up as many dormant ladders as you like and swap which one is live. If you ever drop from Gold back to Free, your advanced configuration isn't deleted — it goes dormant, and re-subscribing brings it straight back. Paid features are marked in the admin with a lock and a link to upgrade, and a badge always shows your current plan.

11. The settings page

Sam's admin is designed so the ladder itself is the star. Here's how to read it.

  • Horizontal ladders — each ladder reads left-to-right (base → tiers), and ladders stack down the page.
  • Compact cards — cards are kept tight so the shape of the ladder is clear at a glance; the base card has a thicker border to mark it as the anchor.
  • Live banner preview — three bands (Intro / Teaser / Win) show exactly what shoppers will read, and update when you select a card.
  • Click to peek — click any card to preview it without opening it for editing.
  • Dormant muting — ladders that aren't live are faded, so the active one stands out.

12. Under the hood

Mostly invisible, but worth knowing Sam handles it properly.

  • Automatic discounts — Sam uses Shopify's own discount functions to apply order and free-shipping rewards at checkout.
  • Settings sync — your admin configuration drives the storefront banner automatically.
  • Privacy — Sam includes the required data-handling webhooks and doesn't collect your customers' personal information.
  • Plan detection — Sam reads your active subscription to know whether you're on a trial, Gold, or free.
